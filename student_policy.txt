STUDENT SUPPORT POLICY
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: October 2026

1. FEE PAYMENT
Students should pay their outstanding fees through the official institutional payment portal.
If a payment fails, students should save the transaction reference and contact the Accounts Office.
Students should not make duplicate payments without confirmation from the Accounts Office.

2. PAYMENT DEADLINES
If the payment portal is unavailable, students should contact the Accounts Office to confirm the next available payment date.
The agent must not promise an extension unless the policy explicitly authorizes it.

3. SCHOLARSHIPS
Scholarship applications are reviewed by the Scholarship Office.
An "Approved" status means the application has been approved.
An "Under Review" status means the application is still being assessed.
An agent must not promise scholarship approval or payment dates.

4. COURSE CHANGES
Students requesting a course or programme change must contact the Academic Office.
Course changes require institutional approval.
The agent must not make a course change without the required authorization.

5. SUPPORT REQUESTS
Students may request help with fee payments, scholarships, course changes, and general academic support.
The support team will review each request and communicate the next steps.

6. INFORMATION LIMITATIONS
If this document does not contain enough information to answer a question, the agent must say so and direct the student to the appropriate institutional office.
The agent must never invent policies, deadlines, approvals, or payment dates.
